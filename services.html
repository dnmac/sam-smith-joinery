Pricing

How quotes and prices work

Every job is quoted as a fixed price after a site visit. Sam does not work to a day rate on domestic jobs, because it is you who ends up carrying the risk if something takes longer than expected.

The quote separates materials from labour so you can see exactly where the money goes, and it holds for 60 days. If you change your mind mid-job that is fine — it gets re-quoted in writing before any extra work starts.

Small repairs and half-day jobs are charged at a minimum callout, which is confirmed before Sam books you in.

Rough guide prices

Genuine ballparks for a typical Leeds semi. Yours will differ — that is what the free visit is for.
