What Sam does

Bespoke joinery and everyday carpentry

From a full fitted kitchen down to a door that has never closed properly, it is the same joiner who quotes the job and does the work.

Fitted wardrobes & bedroom furniture

Made-to-measure wardrobes, drawers and dressing areas built around the room you actually have — including awkward eaves and chimney breasts.

  • Hinged, sliding or walk-in
  • Soft-close doors and drawers
  • Painted or sprayed to any finish

Kitchen fitting & worktops

Full kitchen installations or a careful refit of the units you already have, with worktops templated, cut and joined on site.

  • Solid wood, laminate and composite tops
  • Appliance cut-outs and end panels
  • Works alongside your plumber and sparky

Staircases & bannisters

New flights, replacement spindles and handrails, or a tired staircase brought back to life without ripping the whole thing out.

  • Oak, pine and painted finishes
  • Building-Regs compliant rise and going
  • Creak and wobble repairs

Doors, frames & hanging

Internal and external doors supplied, trimmed and hung properly, so they close first time and still do in a year.

  • Fire doors fitted to spec
  • Locks, latches and ironmongery
  • Frames, linings and thresholds

Skirting, architrave & mouldings

The trim that makes a room look finished — matched to period profiles or brought bang up to date.

  • Profile matching for older houses
  • Picture and dado rails
  • Panelling and box-outs

Decking, fencing & garden joinery

Solid outdoor timberwork that survives a Yorkshire winter, set out level and fixed to proper foundations.

  • Softwood and composite decking
  • Fence panels, gates and posts
  • Pergolas and planters

Why people call Sam back

A joiner who turns up, quotes straight and finishes the job

Plenty of trades will quote low and disappear halfway through. Sam works on one job at a time, gives you a written fixed price before anything starts, and is the person on site every day — not a rotating cast of subbies.

If a repair will do, he will say so. If a job is outside what he does well, he will tell you that too and point you at someone who does.

More about Sam

What you get as standard

  • A written fixed quote Itemised, valid for 60 days, with nothing added later unless you ask for it.
  • A firm start date Booked in writing, with a realistic finish date rather than an optimistic one.
  • Dust sheets and a clean site Floors covered, tools away and the room swept before Sam leaves each day.

How it works

Four steps from first call to finished job

Call, email or fill in the booking form with a rough idea of the job. Photos help enormously, but they are not essential.

Free site visit

Sam comes out at a time that suits you, measures up properly and talks through the options — including the cheaper ones.

Written fixed quote

A clear itemised price by email within 48 hours, with materials, labour and timings set out separately.

Booked in and built

You get a firm start date, daily updates while work is on, and a final walk-round before anything is signed off.
